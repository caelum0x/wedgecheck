Why isn't Pro/Team checkout live yet?

Wedgecheck is a new product, still pre-revenue. We'd rather tell you that plainly than fake a "Buy now" button with nothing behind it. Join the waitlist and you'll get a real invite the day checkout opens, plus founder pricing for being early.

What counts as "a brief"?

One submitted product URL or description that completes a full research pass and returns a cited report -- the same thing the free form on the homepage already does.

Is Free going away?

No -- Free stays real and usable on its own. Pro/Team exist for people who hit the free rate limit regularly or need team-wide history, not to gate the core product.
